Quick-commerce platforms rely on dense urban networks of hyperlocal fulfillment centers known as dark stores positioned within a two-kilometer radius of dense residential zones. Customer lifetime value is fundamentally governed by thirty-day cohort retention curves and order density per courier delivery route. While introductory promotional discounts drive rapid initial adoption, sustained unit economics demand algorithmic inventory assortment optimization, minimal stock-out rates for daily essentials, and bundled cross-merchandising to raise average order values above the delivery margin threshold.
