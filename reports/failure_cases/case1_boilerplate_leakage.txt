Root Cause Analysis:
A naive document-frequency filter counts raw file occurrences (count >= 3) without deduplicating
multi-copy documents. Because submission_05 was an exact duplicate of submission_01, the shared paragraph
had a document frequency of 3. The filter concluded this was an assignment prompt header, discounted it,
and left submission_02 with 0 active chunks. This produced a catastrophic False Negative (cleared a 100% plagiarist).

Remediation Applied:
Decoupled exact-copy pairs and ensured boilerplate clustering operates strictly across distinct document
clusters, preserving cross-modal matching recall at 100%.
