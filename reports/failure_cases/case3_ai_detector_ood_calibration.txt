====================================================================================
FAILURE CASE 3: OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION CALIBRATION IN STATISTICAL AI DETECTION
====================================================================================
Incident:
Benchmarking the multi-signal AI detector across human-authored synonym rewrites vs.
synthetic modern LLM essays (synthesized by Antigravity / Gemini assistant simulating EV bus charging briefs)
produced counter-intuitive probabilities.

Raw Pipeline Metric Output (from reports/evaluation_results.json):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Document ID | Source Category | Raw AI Score | Perplexity | Burstiness | Assigned Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| submission_04_logistics_rewritten.docx  | Human Paraphrase Rewrite  | 72.8% | 84.12 | 0.28 | HIGH (Peer=50%) |
| submission_14_llm_generated_essay.docx  | Antigravity (Gemini LLM)  | 23.0% | 46.85 | 0.39 | LOW  (Peer=0%)  |
| submission_15_llm_generated_synthesis.pdf| Antigravity (Gemini LLM) | 19.4% | 51.20 | 0.42 | LOW  (Peer=0%)  |
| submission_03_logistics_strategy.docx    | Human Original Baseline  | 28.5% | 72.40 | 0.33 | HIGH (Peer=100%)|
| submission_16_quantum_hardware.pdf       | Human Technical Paper    | 13.6% | 61.10 | 0.36 | LOW  (Peer=0%)  |

Root Cause Analysis:
Statistical AI detectors (e.g. RoBERTa OpenAI detector and GPT-2 causal language model loss)
measure local token transition predictability and burstiness.
1. When a human candidate heavily rewrites text using a thesaurus (as in submission_04 synonym replacement:
   "dependable pharmaceutical thermal supply line demands nonstop temperature tracking..."), the sentence
   structures exhibit uniform length and low lexical diversity, which fools statistical classifiers into
   flagging it as machine-generated (72.8% AI probability).
2. Modern frontier instruction-tuned LLMs produce fluent, multi-clause academic prose with natural vocabulary
   transitions that match human perplexity distributions, causing open-source detectors to underestimate
   their probability (e.g. 19.4% on synthesized multi-page synthesis).

Remediation Applied:
Strict Architectural Guardrail: As documented in config.yaml and enforced in RiskCalculator,
AI content estimation is probabilistic and NEVER allowed to trigger a HIGH integrity risk by itself.
Only substantiated peer-to-peer or external document overlaps can escalate a submission to HIGH risk,
preventing unfair candidate disqualification based on statistical detector noise.
